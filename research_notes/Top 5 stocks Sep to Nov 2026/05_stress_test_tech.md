## Q1. Cross-cutting bear context (applies to all 8)

### Takeaway

- **Sep 24:** Oracle issued a force-majeure notice on the Stargate "Project Jupiter" data center.
- **Sep 25:** the Akamai–Anthropic deal hit security stocks.
These land on top of the most-crowded-trade status of semis and a 5.2% 10-year yield. The window's biggest AI-hardware binary is Micron on Sep 30, on day 3, and it sets the tone for DELL, P, NVDA and ANET too.

### Cited Findings

  - Oracle sent a force-majeure notice to a Blue Owl unit on the New Mexico "Project Jupiter" Stargate data center, citing power delays.
  - The Energy Transfer gas pipeline slipped about 6 months, to Feb 1, 2027, after permit denials.
