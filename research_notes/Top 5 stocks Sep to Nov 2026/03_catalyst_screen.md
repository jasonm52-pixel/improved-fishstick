## Q1. Which off-cycle / late Jul–Sep 2026 reporters delivered beat-and-raise with strong reactions and drift?

### Takeaway
The cleanest beat-and-raise-and-drift names are **Dell** (FY27 revenue guide raised to $192B from $165–169B; +15% on Sep 2 and +4.9% on Sep 3; now in the S&P 100), **Snowflake** (FY27 product revenue guide raised to $6.07B from $5.84B; +16.6% day one to a record), **CrowdStrike** (record net new ARR; guidance raised; roughly +11–15%), and **Everpure** (FY28 preliminary guide well above consensus; +18% on Sep 24; joined the S&P 500 Sep 21). ### Inferences
- **PEAD is strongest where the guide raise was large relative to consensus and the stock held its gap:** Dell (a guide about 15% above the prior range; a two-day gain of about 20%), Snowflake (the largest acceleration in 13 quarters), CrowdStrike (holding "near yearly high" a month later), and Everpure (a fresh gap on Sep 24 with estimate revisions still in progress). This is the classic drift profile of upward revisions plus a new high.
- **The AI-hardware "beat but sell" pattern (AVGO, ORCL, CIEN, NVDA's reaction) is a warning.** Positioning in AI infrastructure is crowded (see the regime file). The Oracle force-majeure news on Sep 24 is a new crack in the AI-capex financing chain, and it touches Bloom Energy and possibly other Oracle suppliers.
- Industrials that beat and raised in July (EME) have not drifted. Their high-rate sensitivity and data-center crowding may explain this.
- Cintas is the low-beta, quality beat-and-raise option in a non-AI sector, but its muted reaction implies limited drift.
