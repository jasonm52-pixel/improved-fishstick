## 2. EPS estimate revisions and earnings expectations (FactSet, Sep 2026)

### Takeaway
Q3 2026 S&P 500 EPS growth is expected at about **29%** (up from 26.7% on June 30). That would be a **third straight quarter of 25%+ growth**, on revenue growth of about 11.9%. **Energy (~+76% to ~+110% EPS growth) and Info Tech (~+63%, semis/equipment ~+126%)** lead expected growth, followed by Comm Services (~+51%, Meta-driven). Q3 estimates were revised **up** during the quarter (+1.2–1.4%), led by Energy, IT and Financials. Materials had the largest cuts. Positive guidance is unusually strong (72 positive vs 42 negative). ## 5. Sensitivity to a Hormuz reopening (oil down) and to the 10-year yield

### Takeaway
A **Hormuz reopening** helps airlines, cruise lines, transports, consumer discretionary, chemicals and materials (lower input costs), and probably rate-sensitives via lower inflation and yields. It hurts **Energy (E&Ps, integrateds), defense primes and tanker/oil-services**. **Rising 10-year yields** (5.2%) hurt Utilities, homebuilders, REITs, small caps and consumer discretionary. Banks benefit only from curve steepening. Evidence of this sensitivity: airlines +6–8% and LMT/RTX −3% in the week of the Iran offer, and Utilities −3.5–3.9% in the week the 10-year hit 5.2%.
